# PaTo (Pay Together) — Design System & Visual Identity
> Identity: Social financial micro-tool for young communities & Gen-Z hangouts
> Style Language: Crisp editorial fintech, clean vector spots, high-contrast cobalt & slate
> Dials: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

## 1. Palette
- Primary Brand: `#2563eb` (PaTo Cobalt Blue)
- Accent Tint: `#eff6ff` (Subtle Blue Surface), `#dbeafe`
- Deep Canvas / Text: `#0f172a` (Slate 900)
- Soft Ink: `#475569` (Slate 600)
- Hairline Border: `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200)
- Semantic Status:
  - Success / Lunas: `#059669`
  - Warning / Sisa Porsi: `#d97706`
  - Error: `#e11d48`

## 2. Typography
- Headings & UI: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif
- Numbers, Currencies & Receipts: `Space Mono`, monospace

## 3. Anti-Slop Directives
- NO generic 3D blob characters or distorted AI illustrations.
- NO em dash (—) in UI copy.
- NO fake statistics (10k+ users, 99.9% uptime).
- Touch target minimum 44px on all mobile interactive controls.
- Text contrast strictly follows WCAG AA (>= 4.5:1).
